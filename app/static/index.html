🟣 Algorithm: DBSCAN (Density-Based Spatial Clustering of Applications with Noise)

DBSCAN Density & Noise Dashboard

DBSCAN clusters data based on spatial density without assuming spherical shapes. Normal sessions pack tightly into high-density neighborhoods. Isolated or anomalous attacks that lack neighbor support within radius ε = 1.5 are identified as unclustered Noise (-1).

🟢 Algorithm: KNN (k-Nearest Neighbors Distance Check)

KNN Anomaly Detection Dashboard

KNN measures the average distance of each new packet to its k=3 nearest normal neighbors in feature space. Legitimate traffic stays nestled closely with other normal traffic, while attacks exhibit a stark jump in nearest-neighbor distance.
